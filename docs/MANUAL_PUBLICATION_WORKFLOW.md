# Manual Publication Workflow

Public publication is manual only.

1. Confirm required consent is present.
2. Confirm optional public-summary consent is true.
3. Check the record against docs/DATA_BOUNDARY.md.
4. Remove or coarse-grain any sensitive or identifying detail.
5. Confirm the summary does not imply a controlled validation study, regulatory claim, or performance guarantee.
6. Manually copy the reviewed summary into FactorForge/VALIDATION.md.

ValidationHub must not auto-publish, auto-sync, or push summaries to FactorForge.

# Consent Policy

Required consent:

1. Submitter confirms they have the right to share the information.
2. Submitter confirms no prohibited or confidential data is included.
3. Submitter permits internal FactorForge development use.

Optional consent:

1. Submitter permits a summarized non-confidential result to be included in public VALIDATION.md candidate entries.

Public export is forbidden unless optional public-summary consent is true.

Even with public-summary consent, public release requires manual human review before copying to FactorForge/VALIDATION.md.

Consent language and schema fields must remain aligned with docs/DATA_BOUNDARY.md.

# Public Validation Log Policy

FactorForge/VALIDATION.md is the only public-facing validation artifact.

Entries must be manually reviewed, non-confidential, and supported by optional public-summary consent. ValidationHub must not automatically publish, sync, or push public validation summaries.

Public validation summaries are user-submitted wet-lab feedback summaries. They are not controlled validation studies, regulatory claims, or guarantees of FactorForge performance; they are not guarantees.

Positive, negative, equivalent, reduced, failed-expression, and inconclusive outcomes are all useful when presented as public-safe summaries.
